Best Of NCS - No Copyright Songs

Music has become an essential part of our daily lives, and with the rise of digital platforms, accessing music online has never been easier. This project introduces a free music streaming website where users can listen to non-copyrighted songs without any restrictions. Unlike many paid streaming services, this platform allows users to enjoy music without the need for subscriptions or licensing concerns.


The website provides a simple and intuitive interface, enabling users to browse through a list of available songs and play them with basic controls such as play, pause, forward, and backward navigation. The primary goal is to offer a seamless and engaging listening experience while ensuring that all music available on the platform is legally free to use.
